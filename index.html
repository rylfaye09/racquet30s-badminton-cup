Rules & Guidelines

General Conduct

Be ready on time, respect partners, opponents and officials, and keep play friendly.

Scoring

Rally-point system. Round-robin matches are to 25. Finals follow the cup's best-of-3, 25-point format.

In & Out

A shuttle touching a boundary line is in. A shuttle landing completely outside is out. Players do not make their own in/out calls.

Service

Serve diagonally into the correct service court. Both sides should be stationary and ready before service. Service faults are decided by the match official.

Umpire & Line Judges

The umpire controls the match. Assigned line judges make in/out calls for their lines. Players should raise disputes through the umpire rather than calling the shuttle themselves.
